Why ShadowScan?

Blazing Fast

Written in Bash with fearless concurrency. Leverages asynchronous networking to scan thousands of subdomains in seconds.

Stealth & Proxy Support

Built-in jitter, rate limiting, and HTTP/SOCKS proxy rotation to keep your scans undetected and bypass restrictions.

Multi-Format Output

Export your valuable findings seamlessly to JSON, CSV, or plain text to pipe into your other security tools.

Smart Wordlists

Supports massive custom wordlists, permutations, and intelligent auto-detection of wildcard DNS to reduce false positives.
